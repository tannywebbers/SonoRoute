ANDROID LIMITATIONS

1. Third-Party App Stream Isolation: Android security sandboxing prevents non-system applications from universally intercepting or modifying audio streams belonging to external apps (such as Spotify, YouTube, WhatsApp, or games). SonoRoute manages SonoRoute-owned audio streams, communication-mode routing (MODE_IN_COMMUNICATION), and platform-compliant capture.
2. Bluetooth Codec Latency: End-to-end Bluetooth latency is dictated by hardware Bluetooth chipsets, Android Bluetooth stack negotiation, and codec profiles (SBC/AAC). SonoRoute calculates and surfaces "Estimated Buffer Latency" rather than unverified end-to-end latency.
3. AudioPlaybackCapture Permissions: Capturing internal playback requires Android 10+ (API 29+) and requires the target application's manifest to permit capture via android:allowAudioPlaybackCapture="true".
4. Microphone Exclusivity: Android hardware microphones cannot be simultaneously shared across conflicting active recording pipelines without HAL collision. SonoRoute implements internal resource locking (MicrophoneResourceState) to prevent resource contention.
