  - Distinct separation between:
    A. SonoRoute-owned audio streams and direct low-latency pipelines
    B. Android communication-device routing (MODE_IN_COMMUNICATION / setCommunicationDevice)
    C. System-controlled routing (OS-managed fallback)
    D. Isolated third-party sandboxed applications (Spotify, YouTube, WhatsApp)
    E. AudioPlaybackCapture limitations (API 29+ target app opt-in / MediaProjection consent)

• Audio Stability, Self-Healing & Glitch Recovery:
  - Real-time AudioTrack.underrunCount tracking flagging buffer instability when underruns >= 2.
  - Interactive self-healing banner allowing users to step up the audio buffer to the next preset (increaseBuffer) with a single tap.
  - Profile switching confirmation dialog preventing accidental interruption of active sessions.
  - Acoustic feedback warning protection preventing dangerous speakerphone monitoring loops.

• Persistent Routing Across Instances:
  - SharedPreferences storage ("sonoroute_routing_prefs") saving preferred input/output endpoints (hardware type, device name, peripheral MAC address).
  - Automatic restoration of user-preferred routes upon peripheral reconnection or app relaunch.

• Privacy & Security Pass:
  - 100% on-device local execution: no network transmission, no analytics, no advertising, and no telemetry.
  - No persistent audio recording storage.
  - Microphone monitoring disabled by default; audio buffers in memory are freed immediately on stream stoppage.
  - Permissions strictly minimized to RECORD_AUDIO, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, and MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS.

• Battery & Resource Efficiency:
  - Zero background audio monitoring, polling, or RMS calculation when idle.
  - Explicit releaseAll() invoked on ViewModel destruction releasing AudioRecord, AudioTrack, communication devices, and audio focus.

6. KNOWN ANDROID PLATFORM LIMITATIONS
-------------------------------------
1. Third-Party App Routing: Android does not permit third-party applications to universally override the audio routing of external sandboxed applications (e.g. forcing YouTube to play through a specific peripheral when the OS or user selected another). SonoRoute transparently documents and communicates this boundary.
2. AudioPlaybackCapture: Requires API 29+ and target applications must have android:allowAudioPlaybackCapture="true" in their manifest or explicit user MediaProjection consent.
3. Bluetooth Codec Latency: Bluetooth hardware (SBC, AAC, LDAC, aptX) introduces unavoidable hardware/codec transmission buffer delay (typically 40ms–180ms) regardless of client application buffer settings.
