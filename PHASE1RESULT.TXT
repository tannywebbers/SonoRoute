4. KNOWN ANDROID LIMITATIONS IDENTIFIED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Permission Requirements: Android requires RECORD_AUDIO to expose and query hardware microphones. Without it, input device enumeration is restricted.
- Bluetooth Device Names: On Android 12+ (API 31+), BLUETOOTH_CONNECT permission is required to read friendly Bluetooth accessory names; without it, names default to fallback category labels.
- Device Address & Capabilities: Many built-in speakers and microphones do not report fixed sample rate lists (returning empty arrays from the HAL), indicating they operate at the system native mixing rate or flexible hardware sample rates.
- Global Routing Restriction: Third-party Android apps cannot globally hijack audio streams belonging to other independent applications. System-controlled vs application-controlled routing must remain explicitly distinct.
